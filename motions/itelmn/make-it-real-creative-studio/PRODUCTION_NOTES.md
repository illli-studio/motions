# Production notes

## Story beats
- 0–2s: A short Chinese instruction is typed. The pointer submits it.
- 2–6s: Three 2D guide paths release idea packets; a physical creative workspace opens.
- 6–10s: Fast local push toward the form station; projected vector control handles are tethered to the helper's hand.
- 10–14s: Camera travels to the sound station. 3D bars and a 2D waveform envelope are driven by the actual finished soundtrack peaks.
- 14–18s: Timeline clips assemble in the physical work surface; camera rises to show the edit.
- 18–21s: The finished player contains actual earlier footage, perspective-mapped into its 3D screen.
- 21–24s: The player resolves to MAKE IT REAL; the final Chinese message rests in negative space.

## Review corrections
1. Removed repetitive large lower-third panels and corner HUD, which made the first design feel templated.
2. Separated background color from 3D exposure; preserved a cream environment without clipping the character and shape highlights.
3. Corrected the folding canvas's code-line transforms, removed floating-line artifacts, and moved audio graphics clear of the character's face.
4. Replaced approximate construction marks with actual camera-projected cube corners and hand endpoint.
5. Replaced static final play icon with actual film playback and an animated 2D closing screen.

## Honest boundaries
- Real Blender geometry and camera; Workbench stylized shading, not path tracing.
- Original score and sound design; measured loudness/peak checks, no certified human listening pass.
- Hyperframes HTML assembly supplied. Final MP4 is Blender/Python/FFmpeg output, not an official Hyperframes CLI render.
- No AI-generated external imagery was necessary for this concept. Mixed media means choosing what serves the piece, not adding every technique mechanically.
