# A Little More Light
## Style Prompt
A nocturnal product ballet: a warm ceramic articulated lamp unfurls against a plum-black stage, uncovering two engraved botanical islands. Real mesh geometry moves with measured mechanical weight; crisp vector-like botanicals and fine pools of light provide detail. One continuous camera, no cuts.
## Colors
- #201e32 midnight plum background
- #eac8a5 warm porcelain lamp
- #ae775c copper joint rims
- #b6d2b1 sage botanical leaves
- #f6dc91 golden illumination
## Typography
DejaVu Serif, 64px only for small opening/end title; DejaVu Sans Mono reserved for preview controls, no explanatory text.
## Motion
0–2 quiet silhouette; 2–5 articulated extension; 5–10 first garden; 10–14 light sweeps; 14–19 second garden; 19–24 settled warm tableau. Quintic motion with gentle held moments, not perpetual orbit.
## What NOT to Do
No robots or anthropomorphic eyes. No video plate. No literal narrated text, excessive bloom, rainbow gradients, flat fake-3D illustration, or camera motion that hides the action.
