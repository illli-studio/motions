# A Place for Everything
## Style Prompt
A tactile industrial-design miniature on cool porcelain. Coral, ultramarine and butter-yellow tokens find exact homes in a three-part organizer. Crisp technical ink diagrams become solid beveled objects. Smooth camera rotation, short precise hops, and a long resolved hero hold. No simulated application or brand claims.
## Colors
- Background #e9edf5
- Ink #23314a
- Coral #e87764
- Ultramarine #495bc4
- Butter #e4bd5c
- Porcelain tray #f7f3e9
## Typography
DejaVu Serif for one closing title, DejaVu Sans Mono for tiny editorial chapter marks only. Main storytelling is geometry.
## Motion
24 seconds at 100 BPM. 0–3 flat scattered marks; 3–6 solid extrusion; 6–9 trays assemble; 9–15 nine synchronized hops; 16–19 trays dock; 19–24 slow hero orbit. Every position is an analytic function of timeline time, including jumps and camera.
## What NOT to Do
No robots, flowers, prisms, cups, torus rings, ribbons, stock imagery, video backgrounds, flashing effects, fake UI or unsupported technical claims.
