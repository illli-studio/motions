# Split Second: A Journey Through Light
## Style Prompt
A museum instrument becomes an immersive light sculpture. Bone-white technical paper, graphite hairlines, translucent icy glass and a controlled spectral sequence. The prism is a true geometric solid, not a raster plate. Tight macro moves contrast with calm held diagrams. Optical paths are art-directed, not a physical ray-tracing simulation.
## Colors
- #f3efe6 ivory gallery background
- #183b49 deep petrol type and structures
- #b6d9df icy glass faces
- #ff6c62 coral end of spectrum
- #7468d9 violet end of spectrum
## Typography
DejaVu Serif display (museum statement) + DejaVu Sans Mono labels (instrument notation), both locally licensed.
## Motion
0–4 seconds diagram; 4–8 geometric extrusion; 8–12 dispersion; 12–17 macro spectral travel; 17–20 settle; 20–24 title resolution. 120 BPM. Ease-in/out camera, short fast connective moves, no random frame state.
## What NOT to Do
No dark tech dashboard, no fake numerical physics, no equations implying simulation accuracy, no stock video, no glitter particles, no permanently rotating object, no text over the main beam.
