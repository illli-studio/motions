# The Last Tram
## Style Prompt
A jewel-like midnight city miniature grows out of a transit diagram. Lavender masonry, indigo water, warm amber tram and lit apartment windows. Perspective geometry, restrained crisp illustration, purposeful camera moves with readable holds. Transit map becomes physical rails; destination becomes a welcoming pool of light.
## Colors
- #101327 midnight backdrop
- #7478ac lavender architecture
- #343d69 deep structural faces
- #ffbd65 amber tram and windows
- #e8e3da warm typography
## Typography
Bundled DejaVu Serif for editorial title; DejaVu Sans Mono for transit annotations.
## Motion
24 seconds at 120 BPM. 0–4 map, 4–8 city rises, 8–16 journey with close tracking, 16–20 close-tracked arrival with opening station threshold, 20–23 motivated pullback, 23–24 final city/title hold. Deterministic 3D world geometry, flat 2D editorial overlays.
## What NOT to Do
No robot, no flower, no ring sculpture, no fake statistics, no full-video runtime asset, no frantic unreadable text.
