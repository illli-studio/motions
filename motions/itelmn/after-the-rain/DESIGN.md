# After the Rain
## Style Prompt
A small cinematic still life: one cobalt umbrella becomes a temporary roof for a rain puddle, then rests as late peach sunlight returns. Sculpted 3D cloth, fine brass ribs, blue wet terrazzo, elliptical graphic ripples; spacious warm editorial composition. One continuous gently orbiting shot, no cuts.
## Colors
Cobalt #2447a8 canopy; midnight #25304c street; dusty peach #eab7a4 returning light; butter #ffe0a8 sun; silver #b9c9d8 rib highlights.
## Typography
DejaVu Serif title, DejaVu Sans Mono small editorial line. Title only; no explanatory captions.
## Motion
0–3 closed umbrella arrives; 3–7 opening runner rises and ribs spread; 7–13 showers dance; 13–18 warm clearing; 18–21 folding and careful lowering; 21–24 rest. Smooth quintic easing, stronger opening accent, slower last hold.
## What NOT to Do
No robot, no flower, no abstract ribbon; no infographic panel; no abrupt cuts; no fake pre-rendered plate; no uncontrolled simulation or random playback; no shaft through fabric.
