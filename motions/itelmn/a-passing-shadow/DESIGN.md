# A Passing Shadow
## Style Prompt
A quiet astronomical theatre in deep ink, warm ivory sunlight, dusty lilac moon and sea-glass miniature world. Geometry and thin orbital drafting lines tell one continuous alignment, shadow passage and return of light. Deliberately not to scale; no scientific simulation claim.
## Colors
Ink #171e32; ivory #ffe3a8; lilac #9b93b0; sea glass #73aaa8; contour #b8d7cf.
## Typography
DejaVu Serif for a brief museum-like title; DejaVu Sans Mono for restrained not-to-scale caption. Bundled licensed fonts.
## Motion
Ease into alignment, slow passage at the centre, then generous return of light. No strobing. Continuous camera drift.
## What NOT to Do
No literal numerical science claims. No video plates. No flashing solar pulses. No robotic characters. No texture downloads.
