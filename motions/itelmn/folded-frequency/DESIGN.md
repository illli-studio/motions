# Folded Frequency
## Style Prompt
An editorial sound sculpture: a fine cobalt signal acquires volume as a coral and ice-blue metallic ribbon, floating in a midnight exhibition space. Deliberate camera accelerations counter long weightless holds. Typesetting is calm and precise, never dashboard clutter.
## Colors
- #101621 — midnight background
- #f4ead8 — ivory typography
- #ff795e — coral front surface
- #73b9db — blue reverse surface
- #a9cfff — traveling energy
## Typography
DejaVu Serif display and DejaVu Sans Mono microtype; bundled and licensed.
## What NOT to Do
No photorealism claim, no stock video, no neon UI boxes, no robot or mechanical motifs, no random particles, no full-screen gradient.
