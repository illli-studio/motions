# Sympathetic Motion
## Style Prompt
An intimate acoustics sculpture in a wine-black gallery. Two ivory tuning forks on dark glazed plinths answer one another with hairline apricot waves. Real extruded and shaded geometry, a visible brass mallet, an orbiting camera, and deliberate quiet intervals. The image tells the story, not explanatory captions.
## Colors
- #241c2b wine-black gallery
- #f3dfc6 warm ivory resonators
- #d59963 brushed brass striker
- #ee927e apricot vibration traces
- #7c9292 desaturated celadon secondary fork
## Typography
DejaVu Serif for the sparse gallery title; DejaVu Sans Mono for a tiny edition caption. Title only in opening and closing, not over the sculpture.
## Motion
One continuous camera move. Slow introduction, percussive strike at 3.6s, wave arrival at 7.2s, accelerating duet at 10–17s, calm decay and final wide at 21s. Vibration is deliberately slowed and exaggerated, not a simulation or claim of exact sound propagation.
## What NOT to Do
No robots, flower/ribbon/torus imagery. No fake numerical physics. No video textures. No dense charts. No visible explanatory paragraphs. No random or stateful integration.
