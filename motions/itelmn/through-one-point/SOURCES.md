# Sources and scientific boundaries
- Australian National University, Make a pinhole camera: https://science.anu.edu.au/engagement/science-lab-experiments-home-school/make-pinhole-camera — light-tight box, internal screen, top-bottom and left-right inversion; larger aperture is brighter but blurrier.
- MIT Vision Book, Imaging: https://visionbook.mit.edu/imaging.html — pinhole geometry, straight-line projection; geometrical ray model ignores diffraction.
- University of Illinois optics exercise: https://courses.physics.illinois.edu/phys508/fa2016/508hw2.pdf — homogeneous medium has position-independent refractive index; straight rays.
