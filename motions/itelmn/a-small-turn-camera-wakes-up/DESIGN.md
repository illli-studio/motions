# A Small Turn: The Camera Wakes Up
## Style Prompt
A tactile fictional compact camera becomes an instrument for noticing a small still life. Burgundy leatherette, brushed warm silver, deep teal anti-reflection glass and cream studio paper. Oversized, precise mechanical details contrast with quiet editorial typography. No existing brand or product is depicted.
## Colors
Cream #eee7da; burgundy #743441; silver #c6bdb0; teal #397c79; ink #302c32.
## Typography
DejaVu Serif for statements and DejaVu Sans Mono for mechanical labels, both bundled with license.
## Motion
24 seconds,120BPM. 0–4 focus diagram;4–8 assembly;8–12 lens focus turn;12–15 iris macro;16 shutter;17–20 photographic card;20–24 final resolve. Camera translations and depth are interpolated, not canned rotations.
## Avoid
No brand replicas, fake specifications, robots, persistent dashboard, illegible microtype, static central object for most of film, or raster/video plates.
