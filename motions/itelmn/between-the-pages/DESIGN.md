# Between the Pages
## Style Prompt
An intimate bookbinding atelier: ivory paper, oxblood cloth, petrol-blue ink and warm brass. A sculptural hardcover opens into two small printed worlds. Flat, original vector-like landscape marks become part of genuinely rotating page geometry. A saffron ribbon finds its resting place; the closing cover preserves the journey. No narrative captions or interface panels.
## Colors
- #eee7d9 warm paper backdrop
- #713b45 oxblood cloth cover
- #173d49 petrol ink and shadows
- #e6b451 saffron bookmark and sun
- #779c94 quiet sage hills
## Typography
DejaVu Serif display only, 64px, title at first/last moments. No body copy.
## Motion
24 seconds. Cover opens 2–5s. Illustration builds 5–8s. Ribbon enters 8–11s, travels 11–17s, settles 17–19s. Cover closes 19–22s. Smooth quintic camera and physically continuous hinged covers. Sparse plucked score accents each story beat.
## What NOT to Do
No text explaining the story; no robots; no paper flowers; no pre-rendered frames; no generic cards; no jump cuts; no excessive camera spin.
