# Over and Under
## Style Prompt
A warm gallery study of rigid interleaving slats, not cloth simulation. Persimmon rails alternate levels; sea-glass crossbars glide through the clearances. Fine ink plans become a real dimensional diamond lattice, revealed by a smooth rising camera. Restrained editorial typography, precise bevels and soft grounding shadows.
## Colors
- #f2ebdf warm gallery backdrop
- #bc4f49 persimmon rails
- #347a77 sea-glass crossbars
- #263f42 deep ink
- #d4ac65 brass alignment dots
## Typography
DejaVu Serif, DejaVu Sans Mono. Title only, no explanatory text in film.
## Motion
24 seconds. 0–3 drawing; 3–8 rail assembly; 8–15 crossbars thread; 15–21 camera lift reveals pattern; 21–24 rest. Quintic acceleration, asymmetric stagger, mechanical clicks precisely timed with landings.
## What NOT to Do
No cloth bending, no intersections, no spinning logo, no video plate, no tiny paragraphs, no claim of physical simulation.
