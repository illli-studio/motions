# Handle with Wonder
## Style Prompt
A miniature architectural surprise inside a warm terracotta parcel on pale blue paper. Carefully bevelled solid geometry, hinged flaps, tiny cyan and apricot buildings, postal routes drawn as elegant thin graphic lines. Slow anticipation, rhythmic opening, close camera discovery, quiet closure.
## Colors
Sky #e9f0f2; parcel #b96a4e; interior #e9cba6; city teal #458a8d; window #ffd784.
## Typography
DejaVu Serif title, DejaVu Sans Mono details, licensed locally.
## What NOT to Do
No robots, flowers, gradient backgrounds, video plates, repeated spinning, text exposition or fake physics claims.
