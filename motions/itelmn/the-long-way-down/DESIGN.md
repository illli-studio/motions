# The Long Way Down — visual identity

## Style Prompt
A tactile museum-scale marble machine in an aubergine night gallery. A pale porcelain S-shaped trough floats above ribbed bronze supports; a luminous mint marble moves from a suspenseful crest into a fast, banked descent, finally striking a suspended brass bell. One uninterrupted, smoothly choreographed camera follows true projected 3D geometry. Flat cream typography and precise mint path/gravity annotations form a restrained editorial layer, not a dashboard.

## Colors
- #251b2b — aubergine gallery background
- #3b2a40 — raised stage and shadows
- #eee2cc — porcelain track and primary type
- #82e2bd — mint marble and path annotations
- #bc8050 — brushed bronze supports and terminal bell

## Typography
Bundled DejaVu Serif for the title/poetic ending, DejaVu Sans Mono for measured annotations. Contrast a museum-label serif with the quiet drafting register of mono. Headlines 64–94px, labels 20–26px.

## Motion
24 seconds, 30fps. One continuous spatial scene with camera moves rather than shot cuts. 0–6.6: slow anticipation. 6.6–15.0: accelerating fall and bank. 15–18.6: runout and brass strike. 18.6–24: ring and settle. Physically inspired, deliberately time-remapped rolling, not a calibrated physics demonstration. The 2D route diagram indicates normalized path progress and a direction-of-gravity arrow, not false scientific measurement.

## What NOT to Do
No robot, camera, paper flower, clockwork ring, tram or prism. No video plates, prerendered frame sequences, external CDNs, stock recordings, WebGL/PBR claims, glows hiding geometry, scientific-looking fabricated units or unrelated HUD clutter.
