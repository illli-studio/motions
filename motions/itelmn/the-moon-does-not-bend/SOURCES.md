# Fact sources — checked 2026-10-27
1. NASA, Moon Phases. https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moon-phases/ — lunar phases are changing views of the sunlit part as the Moon orbits Earth.
2. NASA, Top Moon Questions. https://science.nasa.gov/moon/top-moon-questions/ — ordinary phases are not Earth's shadow; the Moon's sun-facing half receives light except during lunar eclipses.
3. NASA, Eclipses and the Moon. https://science.nasa.gov/moon/eclipses/ — lunar eclipse requires Earth between Sun and Moon with appropriate alignment; tilted orbital plane prevents an eclipse at every full Moon.
