# The Breathing Estuary
## Style Prompt
An editorial coastal terrain miniature in midnight blue, jade water and warm ochre sand. A readable connected channel map slowly tilts into a real geometric landscape. The tide expands and contracts once; luminous flow tracers follow the water, not decorative loops. Quiet confident typography, broad shapes and fine contour lines.
## Colors
- #102b34 deep blue background
- #187c83 deep channel water
- #70c7bd shallow water and flow
- #d6b777 sandbank tops
- #f4e6c9 typography and highlights
## Typography
DejaVu Serif (EstuarySerif), DejaVu Sans Mono (EstuaryMono), bundled licenses.
## Timing
0–3 map, 3–7 camera tilt and rising tide, 7–13 branching surge and high-water hold, 14–20 ebb and contour reveal, 20–24 hero hold.
## What NOT to Do
No literal fluid simulation claims, numeric tidal data, photo/video plates, decorative disconnected channels, chart-like statistics, abrupt cuts or repeated earlier-film objects.
