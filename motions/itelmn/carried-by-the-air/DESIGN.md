# Carried by the Air
## Style Prompt
A quiet botanical sculpture on warm ivory, a copper-winged seed floats from a walnut twig through fine sage airflow contours. Real shaded geometry and engraved veins are counterpointed by precise 2D trails. A continuous camera eases from release to aerial orbit, then descends toward a small soil island. Original warm plucked score.
## Colors
Ivory #f4f0e5; walnut #634531; copper #c78548; pale gold #dfbb75; sage #698575; deep olive #344b42.
## Typography
DejaVu Serif for the small opening/final title, DejaVu Sans Mono for optional editing interface only.
## What NOT to Do
No factual aerodynamic claims. No paper flower or robot. No labels as narrative. No neon. No video plate. No abrupt scene cuts.
## Timing
0–3s attached seed and quiet anticipation; 3–7s release and accelerating autorotation; 7–16s drifting arc; 16–20.5s descent and damped settling; 20.5–24s rest.
