D = rho Cd A v² / 2; at terminal descent D = mg and vt = sqrt(2mg/(rho Cd A)). A is the consistent frontal reference area, not curved canopy cloth area. Equal total mass, constant density and drag coefficient, still air, negligible buoyancy. Area ×4 gives terminal speed ×1/2 only under these modeled conditions. Real canopies deform and Cd changes. Numbers used in source are chosen model parameters, not measurements. From rest, speed grows toward terminal speed asymptotically; do not describe this release as deceleration. At terminal conditions both canopies' drag equals their same weight.
