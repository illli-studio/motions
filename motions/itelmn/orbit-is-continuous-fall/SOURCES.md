# 卫星为什么不会掉下来？ — science sources and limits

Verified against primary institutional sources on 2026-10-27 (UTC). This is a qualitative explanation of an ideal circular Earth orbit, not an orbit-design calculation.

## Verified factual spine

1. **Orbit is continuous free fall, not absence of gravity.** An orbiting spacecraft remains under Earth's gravity; gravity supplies the inward (centripetal) acceleration. [NASA — Chapter 3: Gravity & Mechanics, Freefall](https://science.nasa.gov/learn/basics-of-space-flight/chapter3-4/).
2. **In the ideal model, gravity is the only force.** Free fall is motion under gravity alone. The orbital velocity vector is tangent to the path, while acceleration points inward. Velocity is not a force and is not an opposing force to gravity. [NASA Glenn — Free Falling Objects](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/free-falling-objects/).
3. **A suitable sideways speed allows the falling path to pass around Earth instead of striking the surface.** NASA's cannonball thought experiment describes orbit as continuous free fall at an appropriate speed. [NASA Glenn — What is Microgravity?](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/glenn/what-is-microgravity/).
4. **Gravity remains significant in orbit, while forward motion is inherited from launch.** NOAA's explainer supports this high-level context. Do not reproduce its loose language about momentum and gravity being balanced. [NOAA NESDIS — Why Don't Satellites Fall out of the Sky?](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/about/k-12-education/satellites-technology/why-dont-satellites-fall-out-of-the-sky).
5. **Real orbits need not persist forever.** Atmospheric drag can lower low-Earth satellite orbits; perturbations also exist. [NASA Earth Observatory — Catalog of Earth Satellite Orbits](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/catalog-of-earth-satellite-orbits/).

## Model and visual boundaries

- Earth is a spherical, fixed central body; satellite mass is negligible by comparison.
- The explanatory orbit is ideal and circular. Neglect atmosphere, other gravitating bodies, thrust, radiation pressure and nonspherical gravity.
- No real altitude, speed, period or scale is asserted. Geometry, satellite size, arrow lengths and animation timing are schematic, not to scale.
- Show a velocity arrow tangent to the orbit in the same direction as satellite motion.
- Show a gravity/acceleration arrow radially inward from the satellite toward Earth's center.
- The two arrows represent different physical quantities; their lengths are not a comparison of forces. An on-screen label such as “速度方向” versus “引力方向” avoids that conflation.
- For a circular orbit, speed can remain constant while velocity direction changes; gravity continually changes direction of motion.
- “不断落下，却不断错过地面” is qualitative shorthand for this curved free-fall path, not a claim that orbital radius continually shrinks.
- Do not show a stationary levitating satellite, engine/thrust supporting it, or two equal-and-opposite force arrows.
- Do not claim gravity disappears, is canceled by velocity, or is balanced by momentum. Momentum has units of mass × velocity and is not a force.
- Do not imply all sufficient speeds give a circular orbit: the circular speed is specific to orbital radius. A very fast object can escape; a slower object can enter another orbit or intersect Earth. The film need not discuss these cases.
- The closing conclusion must not claim satellites can never fall back. An unobtrusive caveat should say “理想圆轨道 · 忽略空气阻力 · 示意非比例” or equivalent.

## Editorial handling of simplifications in references

The NASA Chapter 3 and NOAA child-facing prose includes informal “balanced” language. This film intentionally uses the more precise Newtonian statement from NASA Glenn: **gravity is the inward net force; sideways velocity is a state of motion, not a counterforce.** Source authority is not a reason to repeat an avoidable misconception.

## Recommended 28-second content sequence

- Ask: “卫星为什么不会掉下来？”
- Reveal: “其实，它一直在下落。”
- Mechanism: “它也有沿切线的速度；引力不断改变运动方向。”
- Visual inference: “地面向下弯去，它就不断错过地面。”
- Close: “轨道，是绕着地球的自由落体。”
- Persistent/final caveat: “理想圆轨道 · 忽略空气阻力 · 示意非比例”。
