# One More Cup
## Style Prompt
A quiet miniature about making room for company. Rain beyond a tall architectural window; two handmade ceramic cups and a self-moving brass kettle. Cream daylight turns amber. Real projected 3D geometry and gentle 2D rain, steam and editorial type; no people or robots.
## Colors
- #f4eddf warm plaster
- #256b69 glazed teal
- #d6aa59 brass and afternoon light
- #4b3830 tea
- #243f3d dark ink
## Typography
DejaVu Serif for generous literary captions; DejaVu Sans Mono for optional tooling only.
## Motion
24 seconds, 90 BPM, measured sliding arcs and deliberate pouring. Stable camera. Continuous single scene. 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps.
## What NOT to Do
No navy/coral; no tiny labels; no human silhouettes; no flat fake cup ellipses; no pre-rendered plate; no random playback state.
