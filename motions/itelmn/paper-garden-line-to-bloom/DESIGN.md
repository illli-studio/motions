# Paper Garden / 一线生花
## Style Prompt
An editorial botanical sculpture. One fine graphite line travels across warm paper, rises into a folded green stem, and blossoms into coral origami petals. Crisp triangular planes, real paper thickness, delicate linework, tactile quiet percussion. The camera progresses from a plan drawing into a low spatial view and finishes on an architectural garden.
## Colors
- warm paper #F1EBDC
- ink #203A32
- forest #36614D
- sage #9EAF8B
- coral #DF705A
## Typography
Noto Sans CJK Bold and DejaVu Sans. Sparse Chinese editorial text with an English title only at the end. No UI chrome, badges, counters, full-width subtitle bands, or gradients.
## Motion
20 seconds, native 1920×1080, 30fps. Draw → fold → bloom → propagate → settle. Camera accelerates between subjects and settles for the decisive folds; no random shake.

## Implementation and provenance
Real Blender 4.3 Workbench polygonal 3D, native 1920×1080 frames; Pillow camera-projected 2D ink and typography; original NumPy/SciPy music synthesis; FFmpeg video and AAC encoding. The editable Hyperframes HTML project assembles a rendered visual plate with its audio; it is not a claim that Blender geometry is editable in Hyperframes or that the official Hyperframes CLI rendered this MP4. Geometry, motion, drawings and score are original procedural work. Bundled Noto font subset and DejaVu fonts retain their accompanying third-party licenses.
